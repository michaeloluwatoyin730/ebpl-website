FlashPay by ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED

FlashPay is a Nigerian money app. Send money to any bank, top up airtime and data, and pay electricity and TV bills from one place.

Opening soon. Waiting list open

FlashPay is a money app made in Nigeria by ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED. It is built so that everyday payments take a few taps and are easy to follow until they finish.

One 6-digit PIN protects your account and approves every payment, and every payment you make is recorded in your history.

The app is built and being tested. We are finishing the steps needed to open it to the public, and the FlashPay website has a waiting list you can join.
