How ADDY began

A chapter of the founder story

Years ago, he often walked into a well-known superstore, and not only to shop. He went to study. He watched how products were arranged, how the store was structured and how the team ran it day to day. He wanted to understand what made that business model work.

Those visits became part of the journey that eventually gave birth to ADDY. The vision was never to copy an existing business. It was to learn from what already worked and build something with a different way of operating.

One thing he has come to appreciate about entrepreneurship is that you do not always have to start by inventing something completely new. Sometimes you learn from the businesses that paved the way, spot the opportunities they have not explored, and shape something that fits your own vision.

Learn from what is already working. But do not stop at imitation. Build your own.
