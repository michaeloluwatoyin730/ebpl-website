ADDY by ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED

ADDY is a pickup-first online supermarket in Ibafo, Ogun State. Customers browse, order and pay in the app, then collect their groceries at a pickup point.

Being built

ADDY is a technology-enabled supermarket platform by ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED. Customers browse products with live stock, order and pay online, then collect from a single pickup point in Ibafo, Ogun State, Nigeria.

Your order is paid and confirmed before pickup. Simple. Smart. Essential.

ADDY is still being built and is not open to the public yet. This page will link to the ADDY website once it opens.
