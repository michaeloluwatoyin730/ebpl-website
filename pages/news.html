News and Insights

Announcements, updates and insights from ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED and its family of businesses.

Corporate July 2026
ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED Formally Incorporated with the CAC

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED has been formally registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria under registration number RC 9658205. This milestone establishes the legal and corporate foundation for the group's holding company structure and enables the formal launch of each subsidiary under a properly registered entity. The registration reflects the group's commitment to operating with full regulatory compliance from its earliest stages, a principle that will continue to guide every business decision as the group grows.

Technology June 2026
FlashPay Completes Full Application Build Ahead of Q4 2026 Launch

FlashPay, the group's flagship fintech subsidiary, has completed the full build of its mobile application. The platform includes a partner wallet powered by Monnify's CBN-licensed infrastructure, giving every user a dedicated Nigerian bank account on registration. Additional features include instant money transfers, airtime and data purchases, electricity and television subscriptions, a goal-based savings system, a loyalty rewards program called FlashPoints, KYC verification with tiered transaction limits, biometric authentication, and an intelligent risk detection system. Public launch is targeted for Q4 2026 following CAC registration and regulatory sign-off on the compliance architecture.

Retail 2026
ADDY Retail Brand Established Under ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED has formally established ADDY as a retail brand within its portfolio. ADDY is designed to serve everyday consumer needs across Nigeria with a focus on quality, accessibility, and genuine relevance to the Nigerian market. The brand is currently in active development, with market entry planned following the successful launch of FlashPay and the formal establishment of the group's operational infrastructure.

Announcement 2026
Corporate Website Launched at ebusinessplatform.com.ng

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED has launched its official corporate website at ebusinessplatform.com.ng. The website provides a central home for information about the group, its subsidiaries, its values, and its long-term vision. As the group grows, each subsidiary will maintain its own dedicated web presence while linking back to the parent group through the corporate site.
