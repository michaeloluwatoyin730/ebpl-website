About the company

A Nigerian company building FlashPay, ADDY and Vibee from Ibafo, Ogun State.

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED is a Nigerian company based in Ibafo, Ogun State. It was incorporated on 6 July 2026 as a private company limited by shares, with registration number RC 9658205.

The company started with one founder and one Android phone. Its first products, FlashPay and Vibee, were built from that phone, and ADDY is now being built.

Our aim is simple: make everyday things in Nigeria, such as paying, buying and staying in touch, easier and safer to do from a phone.
