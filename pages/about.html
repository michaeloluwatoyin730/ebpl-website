About the Group

Who We Are

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED is a Nigerian holding company founded with a clear and deliberate purpose: to build a portfolio of businesses that create genuine, lasting value for the people they serve.

Our Story

Where We Came From

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED was founded from a simple but powerful conviction: that Nigeria's most pressing challenges are also its greatest business opportunities, and that the companies best positioned to address those challenges are ones built from within the communities they serve.

The group began with an observation about financial exclusion. Tens of millions of Nigerians manage real money, run real businesses, and support real families every day without access to the digital financial tools that could make their lives significantly better. That observation became FlashPay, our first subsidiary, a mobile-first fintech application designed specifically for Nigeria's informal economy.

As the group grew, it became clear that financial technology was only the beginning. The same principles that guide FlashPay, rigorous execution, genuine customer focus, and a long-term perspective, apply equally across other sectors. ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED is the vehicle through which those principles are applied, business by business, sector by sector.

We are not building to sell. We are building to last.
Timeline

Key Milestones

2024
The Group is Founded

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED is established with a clear holding company structure and a mandate to build diversified, high-quality businesses across multiple sectors.

2024 — 2025
FlashPay Development Begins

Development of FlashPay, the group's flagship fintech subsidiary, begins. The application is built from the ground up with a focus on security, usability, and genuine relevance to Nigeria's informal economy.

2025
ADDY Brand Established

The group establishes ADDY, a retail brand designed to serve everyday consumer needs with the same quality and reliability that defines the group's approach to every business.

2026
FlashPay Completes Full Build

FlashPay completes its full application build, including wallet infrastructure, bill payments, savings products, loyalty rewards, and a comprehensive security framework. Public launch is targeted for Q4 2026.

July 2026
CAC Registration Secured

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED is formally incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria under registration number RC 9658205, establishing the legal foundation for the group's holding company structure.

Leadership Philosophy

How We Lead

Great businesses are not built by accident. They are built by teams with clear values, consistent standards, and the discipline to apply both under pressure.

We Lead by Example

The standards we hold our businesses to are the same standards we hold ourselves to. Every commitment we make to our customers, our partners, and our team members is one we expect our leadership to model in their own conduct.

We Decide with Clarity

We make decisions with the information available, acknowledge uncertainty where it exists, and commit fully once a decision is made. We do not let the pursuit of certainty become an excuse for inaction.

We Build for Everyone

The businesses under our group are designed to serve people across income levels, geographies, and backgrounds. Inclusion is not a value we add later; it is a design principle we start with.

We Take Responsibility

When things go well, our customers and our team deserve the credit. When things go wrong, leadership takes responsibility first. That is the only model of accountability we recognise.

Governance

How We Are Structured

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED operates under a formal holding company structure. Each subsidiary is governed independently with its own leadership, operations, and accountability frameworks, while benefiting from group-level strategic oversight.

Holding Company Structure

The group operates through a holding company model in which ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED maintains strategic ownership of each subsidiary while allowing each business to operate with appropriate autonomy.

Regulatory Compliance

Every subsidiary is required to operate in full compliance with applicable Nigerian law and sector-specific regulations. The group actively engages with regulators and views compliance as a competitive advantage, not a burden.

Financial Transparency

The group maintains clear and accurate financial records across all subsidiaries. As the group grows, formal audit and reporting standards will be introduced in line with best practice for companies of our scale.

Stakeholder Accountability

We are accountable to our customers, our employees, our partners, and our investors. Each group has a clear and formal channel through which concerns, feedback, and enquiries are addressed.

Sustainability Commitment

We build businesses that are sustainable in every sense: financially, operationally, and in terms of their impact on the communities they serve. Short-term gains that compromise long-term sustainability are not acceptable to us.

Data Protection

The group and all its subsidiaries are committed to handling personal and financial data with the highest standards of security, privacy, and ethical responsibility. Customer data is never a commodity.

Long-Term Vision

Where We Are Going

Over the next decade, ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED intends to build one of West Africa's most respected business groups by expanding our portfolio across carefully selected sectors, deepening our presence in Nigeria, and extending our reach across the African continent and beyond.

Phase 1
2024 — 2026

Establish the holding company structure, launch FlashPay and ADDY, secure regulatory standing, and build a reputation for quality and integrity in our home market.

Phase 2
2027 — 2029

Scale existing subsidiaries to national market leadership, introduce two to three new business verticals, and begin formal expansion into at least two additional African markets.

Phase 3
2030 and beyond

Establish ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED as a recognised African business group with a diversified portfolio, international partnerships, and a track record of sustainable value creation.
