Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy applies to ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED (RC 9658205) and all subsidiaries operating under the group.

1. Introduction

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED and its subsidiaries are committed to protecting the privacy and security of the personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with our websites, applications, and services.

By using any of our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information that is generated through your use of our services.

Information you provide

  • Full name, email address, phone number and date of birth when you register for our services
  • Identity verification documents such as your BVN, NIN or government-issued identification
  • Financial information necessary to provide our services including account details and transaction records
  • Communications you send to us including enquiries, support requests and feedback

Information collected automatically

  • Device information including device type, operating system and unique device identifiers
  • Usage information including the features you use and the actions you take within our applications
  • Transaction data including the amounts, recipients and timestamps of transactions you initiate
  • Log data including IP addresses, browser type and pages visited on our websites

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for legitimate purposes that are consistent with the reason you provided it to us. These include:

  • Providing and operating the services you have registered for
  • Verifying your identity and complying with applicable know-your-customer requirements
  • Processing transactions and maintaining accurate financial records
  • Communicating with you about your account, transactions and our services
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, money laundering and other illegal activity
  • Improving our services based on how they are used
  • Complying with our legal and regulatory obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With licensed financial partners such as Monnify, Flutterwave and VTpass, to the extent necessary to provide our services
  • With regulatory authorities when required by law or when we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations
  • With professional advisors including lawyers, accountants and auditors under strict confidentiality obligations
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of the business, subject to the acquiring entity agreeing to be bound by this Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission, secure session management, access controls and regular security reviews.

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we implement robust security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as is necessary to provide our services and to comply with our legal obligations. Financial and transaction records are retained for a minimum of seven years in accordance with Nigerian financial regulations. When your information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it securely.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to request deletion of your information where we no longer have a legal basis to retain it. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@ebusinessplatform.com.ng.

8. Cookies

Our websites use cookies to improve your experience and to understand how our websites are used. Please see our Cookie Policy for further details.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a new effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@ebusinessplatform.com.ng or write to us at our registered office: ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED, Ibafo, Ogun State, Nigeria.
