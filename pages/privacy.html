Privacy policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: 29 October 2026. This policy covers the website ebusinessplatform.com.ng. Each product (FlashPay, ADDY, Vibee) has its own privacy policy for its app and website.

1. Who we are

ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED (RC 9658205), Ibafo, Ogun State, Nigeria, runs this website.

2. What we collect

This website has no sign-up, no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you for personal details here.

  • If you message us on WhatsApp, we see your phone number, your name on WhatsApp and what you write. WhatsApp handles that message under its own terms.
  • Our hosting provider records technical logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

3. How we use it

We use your WhatsApp message only to reply to you and to follow up on your question. We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only when the law requires it, or with providers that help us run the website, such as our hosting provider.

5. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where the law allows. Message us on WhatsApp to do this.

6. Our products

FlashPay, ADDY and Vibee collect information when you use them. Read the privacy policy of each product before you use it. The FlashPay policy is on the FlashPay website.

7. Cookies

See our Cookie policy.

8. Changes

We will post any change to this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Message us on WhatsApp, or write to ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED, Ibafo, Ogun State, Nigeria.
